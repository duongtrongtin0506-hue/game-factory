Title (58 chars): **Meal Planner & Auto Grocery List – 4 Weeks, Sheets & Excel**

Plan breakfast, lunch, dinner and a snack for 4 weeks from a **60-recipe library**, see each week's estimated food cost against your budget, and get a **grocery list that builds itself** for the week you pick – every ingredient grouped by aisle, with how many planned meals use it.

## Meal features (each one is a real formula in the file)
- **Recipe library: 60 everyday meals** (10 breakfasts, 12 lunches, 26 dinners, 12 snacks) with servings, editable cost per serving and up to 5 main ingredients – plus 40 blank rows for your own
- **4 week planners:** 7 days × 4 meals, dropdowns from the library
- **Weekly cost** = cost per serving × people you cook for; **budget left** turns red when you go over
- **Auto grocery list:** choose week 1–4; the list compacts itself to only what you need, grouped Produce → Dairy & eggs → Meat & fish → Bakery → Pantry; tick "Got it" and the line greys out
- Ingredient index: how often each ingredient is used per week (handy for batch shopping)

## FAQ
**Are the prices right for my store?** They are editable US estimates – type your own on Recipes and every total updates.
**Can I add my recipes?** Yes, 40 blank rows; they appear in the dropdowns and the grocery list.
**Does it handle quantities?** It counts how many planned meals use each ingredient; it does not convert grams/cups.
