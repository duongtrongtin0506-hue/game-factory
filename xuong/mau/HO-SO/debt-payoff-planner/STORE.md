# Debt Payoff Planner 2026 – Snowball & Avalanche (Google Sheets & Excel)

Title (64 chars): **Debt Payoff Planner 2026 – Snowball & Avalanche, Sheets & Excel**

Type your debts once – balance, APR and minimum payment – plus one extra amount per month. The planner simulates every month for up to 7 years: interest, payment and remaining balance for each debt, and rolls each paid-off minimum onto the next target. You get your **debt-free month, months to go and total interest**, and can switch between Snowball and Avalanche to compare.

## Debt features (each one is a real formula in the file)
- Up to 8 debts with **balance, APR % and minimum payment**
- **Snowball** (smallest balance first) or **Avalanche** (highest APR first) – one dropdown; payoff order recalculates
- **Month-by-month plan:** start balance, interest (balance × APR ÷ 12), payment and end balance per debt
- **Freed-up minimums roll over** to the next target debt automatically
- Each debt's **payoff month** and **interest paid**
- Dashboard: debt-free month, months to go, total interest, total debt, balance chart
- **Payments log** + monthly **actual vs plan**, shortfalls in red

## How to use (3 minutes)
1. Debts tab: type each debt (blue cells), your extra monthly payment and the method.
2. Dashboard: read your debt-free month; flip Snowball/Avalanche to compare months and interest.
3. Each month: log real payments on Payments and see actual vs plan.

## FAQ
**Is the interest exact?** It is the standard monthly estimate (balance × APR ÷ 12); your lender's statement is final.
**Does it work in free Google Sheets?** Yes – it uses only functions both Sheets and Excel support.
**More than 8 debts?** Combine the smallest ones into one line.
**Is this financial advice?** No, it is a planning tool.
