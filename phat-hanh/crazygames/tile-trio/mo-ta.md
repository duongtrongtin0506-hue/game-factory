Title: Three of a Tile

Short description: Pick three matching tiles to clear them before the tray fills up.

Description: Tiles are stacked in layers. Tap a free tile to move it to the tray; three of a kind disappear. Clear the whole board to win, but plan ahead: the tray only holds seven tiles. Undo a move or shuffle the board when things get tight. Every level can be cleared.

How to play: Tap any tile that is not covered by another. Collect three of the same picture in the tray to remove them. The level is lost when the tray is full.

Controls: Mouse or touch: tap the tiles.
