Title: Sprout Merge

Short description: Plant seeds, merge them into bigger crops and fill the orders.

Description: A calm merge and idle garden. Plant seeds, drag two of the same crop together to grow the next one, and deliver what each order asks for. Bigger crops earn coins every second, even while you are away. Discover all ten crops up to the Golden Tree, then start a new season with every coin worth more. Come back daily for a bonus on your first order.

How to play: Tap Plant seed. Drag a crop onto the same crop to merge. Drag the crop an order asks for onto the order card. Drag anything onto Sell to make room.

Controls: Mouse or touch: tap and drag. Keyboard: Space plants a seed, M mutes.

Category: Casual

Tags: merge, idle, farm, garden, casual, relaxing, clicker

Orientation: portrait and landscape. Language: English. Devices: desktop, mobile, tablet.
