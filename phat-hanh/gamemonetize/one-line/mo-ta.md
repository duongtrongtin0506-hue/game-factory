Title: One Line Fill

Short description: Draw one continuous line that fills every cell of the board.

Description: A minimalist one-stroke puzzle. Start from the dot and draw a single line through every cell without crossing your own path. Boards grow from tiny shapes to twisting mazes with holes. Stuck? Use a hint to see the next steps. Every level has a solution.

How to play: Press on the dot and drag through neighbouring cells. Every cell must be visited exactly once. Drag back along the line to undo.

Controls: Mouse or touch: press and drag.
