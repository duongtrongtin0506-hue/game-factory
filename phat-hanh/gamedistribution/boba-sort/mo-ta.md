Title: Boba Sort

Short description: Sort the smiling pearls until every cup holds one colour.

Description: A calm colour-sorting puzzle. Each cup holds four pearls; move the top pearl onto a matching one or into an empty cup until every cup is a single colour. Levels start easy and grow to many colours with only a couple of spare cups. Every level can be solved, and finishing in few moves earns three stars.

How to play: Tap a cup to lift its top pearl, then tap another cup to drop it. A pearl can only land on the same colour or in an empty cup.

Controls: Mouse or touch: tap the cups.

Category: Puzzle

Tags: puzzle, sort, color, ball sort, relaxing, logic, brain
